The [model catalog](/models/overview) is generated from [`GET /models`](/api-reference/endpoint/models/list) every hour. This page explains how the numbers on it are derived, so you can reproduce them and know what they do and don't mean.

## Families and variants

Many model IDs are the same model offered in a different way. The catalog groups them into one family page:

| Variant type | Example | Why it's separate |
|---|---|---|
| Privacy | `z-ai-glm-5-3` and `e2ee-glm-5-3-p` | The E2EE variant runs in an attested enclave and may have different limits |
| Speed | `claude-opus-5-5` and `claude-opus-5-5-fast` | Faster serving at a higher price |
| Mode | `kling-v3-pro-text-to-video`, `-image-to-video`, `-motion-control` | Different inputs and prices |
| Task | `nano-banana-pro` and `nano-banana-pro-edit` | Generation and editing endpoints |

Every variant keeps its own model ID, price and privacy tier. Pass the variant's ID as `model`. Dated snapshots such as `deepseek-v4-flash-0731` stay separate families and appear under **Other versions**.

## Normalized prices

Each modality bills in its own unit. The catalog shows the native unit and one reference price, so models can be sorted and compared.

| Modality | Native unit | Reference price |
|---|---|---|
| Text | Per 1M input and output tokens | **Blended**: `(3 × input + output) / 4`, the 3:1 mix Artificial Analysis uses. The cached-input discount is `1 − cached ÷ input`. |
| Image | Per image, by resolution tier | Price per image at the selected tier (1K, 2K or 4K). Edits add a per-image surcharge for extra inputs. |
| Video | Per clip, by resolution, duration and audio | Price per second and per clip at the selected settings, from a full quote matrix (below). |
| Text to speech | Per 1M characters | Per minute of speech at 150 words (about 825 characters) per minute. |
| Speech to text | Per audio second | Per audio hour and per 1,000 minutes. |
| Music and sound effects | Per track, per second or per duration bucket | Per minute of audio where the model allows it; otherwise per track. |
| Embeddings | Per 1M input tokens | Unchanged. |

<Note>
Reasoning models can spend many more output tokens than non-reasoning models on the same task, so the blended price understates their real cost. Cost per task, measured on benchmark runs, is planned; see [Benchmarks](#benchmarks).
</Note>

### Video pricing

Video models don't publish prices in `GET /models`. At build time, Venice calls [`POST /video/quote`](/api-reference/endpoint/video/quote) for every resolution × duration × audio combination each model supports, at its first listed aspect ratio (aspect ratio does not change the price). The catalog stores the full matrix and derives:

- **Per second**: the median of `price ÷ seconds` across durations at a given resolution and audio setting. Prices are linear in duration for almost every model.
- **Draft clip**: 5 seconds, 720p, silent.
- **Production clip**: 10 seconds, 1080p, with audio.

When a model doesn't offer the requested setting, the catalog uses the closest one it supports and marks the price. Edit, motion-control and upscale modes bill by the length of your source video, so they show **By source** instead of a price. Always call `POST /video/quote` before queueing for an exact amount.

## Privacy tiers

| Tier | What it means |
|---|---|
| **E2EE** | Prompts are encrypted on your device and decrypted only inside an attested hardware enclave. See [TEE & E2EE models](/guides/features/tee-e2ee-models). |
| **TEE** | Inference runs in a hardware enclave with cryptographic attestation. |
| **Private** | Served on infrastructure Venice controls, with zero data retention. |
| **Anonymized** | Proxied to the upstream lab without your identity. The lab may retain prompts. |

A family lists every tier its variants offer. Filtering by a tier shows the variant in that tier, with its own price.

## Reference prompt suite

Image and video models are rendered on a fixed prompt suite, the same prompts for every model of a modality, at default settings. That's what keeps the explorer's gallery comparable from one model to the next.

| Modality | Prompts |
|---|---|
| Image | In-image text, photorealism, instruction following, illustration style |
| Image editing | One source photo, edited three ways: a local change, a restyle and added text |
| Upscaling | A 512 px photo upscaled 2x, beside a bicubic enlargement of the same detail |
| Video | Cinematic landscape, seamless loop, urban cinematic |
| Video editing | One source clip, restyled or extended |
| Video upscaling | A 480p clip upscaled 2x, beside a bicubic enlargement of the same detail |

Most renders come from the model pages on venice.ai. Where venice.ai has none, the docs render the same suite through the Venice API, with video models on the cinematic landscape prompt only, to keep the clips light.

Models without renders are listed under **No reference renders yet**.

## Performance

Text model pages show uptime and time to first token for the last 24 hours, measured on Venice API traffic. The figures are counts and timings of requests, never their prompts or outputs. They're refreshed every hour, and a page stops showing them once they're more than six hours old.

| Figure | How it's measured |
|---|---|
| Uptime | Successful requests as a share of successful and failed ones, shown once a model has 100 requests in the 24 hours, rounded down: a whole percent below 1,000 requests and one decimal from there. 5xx errors, timeouts, streams that break partway, and 401, 402 and 404 responses count as failures. A request that fails over to another provider and succeeds counts as a success. Rate limits (429), geo-blocks (403) and requests rejected as malformed (400, 413, 422) don't count either way. |
| Uptime bars | The same score for each of the 96 fifteen-minute periods: degraded below 95%, down below 80%. Below 100 requests, a bar is degraded or down only when the top of its 95% confidence interval is under the line, so one failure among a few requests doesn't turn it red. Hovering a bar shows its uptime as a whole percent, rounded down, once it has 100 requests; on fewer, the figure would give away how many requests there were. |
| Time to first token | Median and 90th percentile, from the start of inference to the first token of reasoning or output, including any failover. Streamed Chat Completions only. Shown for the last hour, 4 hours and 24 hours, each once it has 50 requests. |

An output speed column, in tokens per second, appears once that figure is published.

Venice will measure the other modalities with synthetic probes, never customer prompts, and publish:

| Modality | Metrics |
|---|---|
| Image | Uptime, generation time for one 1K image (p50 and p95), success rate |
| Video | Uptime, queue time and generation time for a 5-second 720p clip (reported separately), success rate |

## Benchmarks

<Info>
Not published yet. This section describes what the catalog will show.
</Info>

Benchmarks are chosen for the frontier: still discriminative at the top, resistant to contamination, and runnable against the model as Venice serves it. Every score will carry one provenance label, never averaged together:

- **Venice-verified**: run on the Venice endpoint shown on the page.
- **Independent**: run by a third party, usually on the lab's own API.
- **Lab-reported**: published by the model's creator.

Each score also shows the benchmark and harness version, reasoning effort, test date and a confidence interval. Scores older than 90 days, or superseded by a new benchmark version, are flagged as stale.

| Modality | Planned headline set |
|---|---|
| Text | Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, plus Humanity's Last Exam, Terminal-Bench, τ³-bench, AA-LCR, IFBench, AA-Omniscience and a Venice Openness Score |
| Image | Human-preference Elo from the Artificial Analysis and LMArena arenas, GenEval 2 and an over-refusal rate |
| Video | Artificial Analysis text-to-video (with and without audio) and image-to-video Elo, LMArena text-to-video |
| Text to speech | Artificial Analysis Speech Arena Elo, word error rate, time to first audio |
| Speech to text | Artificial Analysis word error rate, Open ASR Leaderboard, speed factor |
| Music | Artificial Analysis Music Arena (instrumental and vocals) |
| Embeddings | MTEB Multilingual, RTEB and a Venice held-out retrieval set |

The set is reviewed every quarter. A benchmark becomes a floor check, no longer a headline, once frontier models saturate it or it fails a contamination audit.

## Machine-readable data

The catalog behind these pages is published as JSON at [`/data/model-catalog.json`](https://docs.venice.ai/data/model-catalog.json) and rebuilt with every hourly sync. It suits comparison, analysis and agents; to pick a model at runtime, call [`GET /models`](/api-reference/endpoint/models/list).

| Field | Contents |
|---|---|
| `version` | Schema version, currently `1` |
| `generatedAt` | ISO 8601 time of the last build that changed the data |
| `counts` | Model and family totals, overall and per modality |
| `providers` | Model makers keyed by slug, with `name` and `logo` |
| `families` | Array of families: `slug`, `name`, `modality`, `task`, `provider`, `description`, `primary` (the default variant's ID), `variants` (model IDs), `created` and `updated` (Unix seconds), `privacy` (every tier offered), `uncensored` and `openWeights` |
| `models` | Object keyed by model ID: `name`, `type` (as in `GET /models`), `modality`, `task`, `variant`, `provider`, `family` (its family's slug), `privacy`, one block of limits and capabilities named for the modality (`text`, `image`, `video`, `audio` or `embedding`), `pricing` in the native unit, `headline` (the reference price above) and `media` (reference renders) |
| `benchmarks` | Benchmark definitions; `null` until benchmarks are published |
| `mediaSuite` | The reference prompt suite, by modality |

Fields without a value are left out. Prices are in USD.

The [performance figures](#performance) are a separate file, [`/data/model-performance.json`](https://docs.venice.ai/data/model-performance.json), also refreshed with every hourly sync:

| Field | Contents |
|---|---|
| `version` | Schema version, currently `1` |
| `source` | Where the figures were copied from |
| `generatedAt` | ISO 8601 time Venice computed the figures |
| `window` | `start` and `end` of the 24 hours, and `barMinutes`, the length of one uptime bar |
| `barCodes` | What each letter in `bars` stands for: `u` up, `d` degraded, `x` down, `n` no data |
| `models` | Object keyed by model ID: `uptime` (percent, rounded down: whole below 1,000 requests a day, one decimal from there), `bars` (one letter per bar, oldest first), `barUptime` (each bar's whole percent, rounded down, in the same order, `null` for a bar under 100 requests, and left out when no bar has one), `ttftMs` (time to first token in milliseconds, `p50` and `p90`, for `1h`, `4h` and `24h`) and, once published, `tokensPerSecond` for the same windows |

## Data sources

The catalog is built from public endpoints you can call yourself:

- [`GET /models`](/api-reference/endpoint/models/list) for specs, capabilities and prices.
- [`POST /video/quote`](/api-reference/endpoint/video/quote) for video prices.
- [`GET /models/traits`](/api-reference/endpoint/models/traits) for defaults such as `default_code`.

Prices are in USD. DIEM is billed at parity.

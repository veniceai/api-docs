## Grok Imagine High Quality API FAQ

### How much does the Grok Imagine High Quality API cost?

$0.06 at 1K and $0.09 at 2K per image. Editing costs $0.06 per edit. Prices are in USD and can be paid in DIEM at parity.

### What is the Grok Imagine High Quality model ID?

Use `grok-imagine-image-quality` as the `model` parameter. Other variants: `grok-imagine-quality-edit` (Edit).

### Is the Grok Imagine High Quality API private?

Grok Imagine High Quality is private: requests run on infrastructure Venice controls with zero data retention, and prompts and outputs are never stored or used for training.

### What resolutions does Grok Imagine High Quality support?

Resolutions: 1K, 2K. Aspect ratios: 1:1, 16:9, 9:16, 3:4, 3:2, 2:3. Prompts up to 7,500 characters.

### Which endpoint does the Grok Imagine High Quality API use?

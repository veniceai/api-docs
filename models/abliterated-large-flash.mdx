Abliterated Large Flash is a large language model by Abliteration AI, available on the Venice API as `abliteration-abliterated-model-large-flash`. Requests are anonymized, so the provider never sees your identity.

Abliterated Large Flash is Abliteration AI's unrestricted multimodal model, based on DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and further abliterated.
